# Ed Staff Hours privacy policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Ed Staff Hours is a Chrome extension for CS 61A course staff. It counts time spent working on the course's Ed discussion forum.

## What it looks at

- The address of the front Chrome tab, only to tell whether it's the course's Ed page, another Ed page, cs61a.org, or something else. Chrome doesn't show it the address of any other site.
- Whether the keyboard or mouse was used in the last 3 minutes (Chrome's idle check).
- Whether a key was pressed in a text box on Ed. It never reads which key, what you type, or anything else on the page.

## What it saves

- Minutes per day (total, typing, and reading), plus short-lived details like the current time away from Ed and unanswered banner questions, which are cleared when Chrome quits.
- All of this stays in Chrome on your computer. Nothing is sent to us, Ed, or anyone else. There are no ads, analytics, or trackers.

## Deleting your data

- Removing the extension deletes everything it saved.

## Changes

- If a future version sends any data off your computer (for example, to a shared staff sheet), this policy will be updated and staff will be told before that version is released.

## Questions

Contact the CS 61A staff member who shared this extension with you.
